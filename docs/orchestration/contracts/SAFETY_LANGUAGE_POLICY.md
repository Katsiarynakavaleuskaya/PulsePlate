**Status:** Canonical policy pointer (wording SoT is centralized)
**Last updated:** 8 February 2026 (PR #691)

## Canonical disclaimer (SoT)

Use the canonical disclaimer: `docs/safety/WELLNESS_DISCLAIMER_CANONICAL.md`.

Do **not** duplicate disclaimer wording in other documents.

---

## Boundaries (CBT-inspired coaching vs therapy)

### Allowed (examples)

- Wellness coaching language (habits, routines, reflection prompts).
- Suggestions framed as options and experiments, not prescriptions.
- High-uncertainty behavior: ask clarifying questions; soften tone; avoid confident claims.

### Forbidden (examples)

- Diagnosis, treatment, cure/prevent claims.
- Crisis/clinical instructions without an explicit escalation policy (runtime scope only).
- Any language positioning the system as a therapist/clinician.

---

## Uncertainty requirement (policy-level)

- For inferential outputs: confidence/uncertainty must be explicit.
- Under low confidence: degrade safely (ask questions; avoid prescriptive advice; reference disclaimer SoT).
