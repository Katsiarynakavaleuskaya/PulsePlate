**This application is wellness/education, not medicine.**

All calculations, interpretations, and recommendations are:

## 🚫 Prohibited Language

**Never use:**
- "diagnoses"
- "treats"
- "cures"
- "medical calculator"
- "recommended by doctors" (unless provable)
- "reduces disease risk" (FDA trigger)
