# AI Report Templates (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly)

Дата версии: 20 февраля 2026 года (`America/New_York`)

## Универсальный Заголовок Для Любого Репорта

```text
Title: [Daily|Weekly|Monthly|Quarterly AI Report – Wellness & AI]
Period: [YYYY-MM-DD | YYYY-Www | YYYY-MM | YYYY-Q#]
Timezone: America/New_York
Prepared for: [Founders/Product/Marketing/Investors]
Prepared by: [Owner]
Goal: [информирование / решение / приоритизация]
Confidence: [High|Medium|Low]
```

## Daily Report Template

### Sections

- Highlights (3-5 сигналов).
- Tech Trends (модели, библиотеки, tooling).
- Wellness AI (кейсы в fitness/health/psychology).
- Easy Entry (3-5 low-capex идей).
- Marketing & Growth Tips (канал + KPI).
- Next Steps (action + owner + deadline).

### Daily Sample (Demo)

```text
Title: Daily AI Report – Wellness & AI – 2026-02-20
Period: 2026-02-20
Timezone: America/New_York
Prepared for: Product + Growth
Prepared by: AI Trend Reporter
Goal: Prioritize next 72h experiments
Confidence: Medium

Highlights:
1) Demo-only signal: short-form UGC keeps higher save-rate than static posts.
2) Demo-only signal: wellness users react better to “one-step action” copy.
3) Demo-only signal: onboarding drop-off still concentrated before first action.

Tech Trends:
- Lightweight retrieval + deterministic guardrails pattern remains dominant.
- Teams prioritize cost visibility before adding new LLM touchpoints.

Wellness AI:
- Action-layer products outperform dashboard-only products in early retention.

Easy Entry:
- Launch one ICP-specific pilot page with a single KPI CTA.
- Ship 2 outreach variants (B2B app / B2C coach).
- Publish one “metric -> action” explanatory post.

Marketing & Growth Tips:
- Track Impressions -> Install rate per ASO subtitle variant.
- Keep one owner per hypothesis and one check date.

Next Steps:
- Owner Growth: run ASO subtitle test by 2026-02-24.
- Owner Product: validate Aha step copy by 2026-02-25.
```

## Weekly Report Template

### Sections

- Highlights (5-7 апдейтов недели).
- Tech Trends (новое + maturity).
- Wellness AI (что применимо в продукте).
- Easy Entry (идеи с ICP и time-to-MVP).
- Marketing & Growth Tips (что сработало).
- Decision Proposals (Start / Stop / Continue).
- Next Steps (owner + срок).

### Weekly Sample (Demo)

```text
Title: Weekly AI Report – Wellness & AI – 2026-W08
Period: 2026-W08
Timezone: America/New_York
Prepared for: Founders + Marketing + Product
Prepared by: AI Trend Reporter
Goal: Decide weekly scale/stop actions
Confidence: Medium

Highlights:
1) Demo-only: ASO variant #2 showed best install-to-activation path.
2) Demo-only: B2B outreach response > generic outbound baseline.
3) Demo-only: users engage more with one-task onboarding language.
4) Demo-only: no critical compliance drift in published copy.
5) Demo-only: social proof block requested by sales in all demos.

Tech Trends:
- More teams publishing claim-hygiene docs with source placeholders.
- Growth teams move from activity plans to KPI hypothesis boards.

Wellness AI:
- Practical “daily action” framing remains strongest conversion narrative.

Easy Entry:
- Expand one winning outreach variant to 2 additional ICP segments.
- Publish one mini case-study with wellness-safe wording.
- Add one founder note tied to product proof.

Marketing & Growth Tips:
- Keep weekly review on 3 hypotheses max.
- Use explicit kill rule for low-performing channels.

Decision Proposals:
- Start: social proof capture template in demo process.
- Stop: generic outreach template without ICP pain.
- Continue: KPI-based ASO/SEO cadence.

Next Steps:
- Owner Sales: collect 2 testimonial drafts by 2026-02-28.
- Owner Growth: finalize KPI board v1 by 2026-02-27.
```

## Monthly Report Template

### Sections

- Highlights (главные изменения за месяц).
- Tech Trends (adoption curve, build vs buy).
- Wellness AI (рост + риски).
- Easy Entry (ниши, ROI horizon).
- Marketing & Growth Tips (каналы и bottlenecks).
- Decision Proposals (Start / Stop / Continue).
- Next Steps (roadmap impacts).

## Quarterly Report Template

### Sections

- Executive Summary.
- Market & Investment Landscape.
- Product/Technology Direction.
- Wellness Risk & Compliance Layer.
- Growth System Review.
- Strategic Bets (with kill criteria).
- Next Quarter Actions.

## Security Notes

- Все рыночные/финансовые цифры — только с source или placeholder.
- Demo-примеры не публикуются как factual performance claims.
- Любые health/psychology формулировки проходят wellness-safe review.

## Marketing & GTM

- Репорты должны быть action-driven, не новостным дайджестом ради дайджеста.
- Каждый вывод должен заканчиваться owner + сроком + метрикой решения.
- Weekly cadence — основной двигатель для GTM адаптации.
