## Manual Review Checklist

- All three locale packs preserve the same seven governed shots.
- Each scene keeps the storyboard timing from its locale `storyboard.json`.
- Line-fit statuses marked `render-risk` require human rendered review before
  any protected upload lane can claim readiness.
- FitChef stays inside the safe area and does not cover headline, support copy,
  chart areas, meal rows, list rows, CTA regions, assistant panels, or status regions.
- Product UI remains the primary visual object in every frame.
- Localized copy does not add pricing claims, result promises, professional-role
  framing, fake UI, or new product capabilities.
- Review notes remain internal review notes, not evidence of export, upload, or
  App Store submission.
